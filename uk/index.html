How your earnings work. Fixed fee pro rata to the videos you post (€500 for 180 videos, i.e. €2.78 per video, capped at €500, or €1,000 if you do two features) + views bonus (€1 per 1,000 Instagram and TikTok views, up to 250,000 views per video) + test reels (€0.50 per 1,000 views) + Spark Ads bonus (€0.25 per 1,000 impressions) + unlocked bonuses.

What counts: your Instagram reels, TikTok videos and Facebook reels. Stories don't count. Facebook views aren't part of the views bonus, but Facebook reels do count as videos.

Test reels don't count towards the fixed fee. They're paid on views only (€0.50 per 1,000 views) and don't unlock milestone bonuses.

Golden milestones: 25K = +€30, 50K = +€75, 100K = +€150, 250K = +€400, once per creator over the whole programme.

Consistency bonus. Each dot is a day. It lights up when your 6 videos of the day are live: 2 on Instagram, 2 on TikTok and 2 on Facebook. Stories and test reels don't count. 30 days in a row = +€100, 60 days = +€250, 90 days = +€500. Missing a day resets the counter.

Payment on the 7th of the following month, based on the final figures. Numbers during the month are provisional: views keep growing.
